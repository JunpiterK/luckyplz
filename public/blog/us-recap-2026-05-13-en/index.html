After both a CPI shock (5/12) and a PPI shock (5/13), the market still sided with "the inflation prints are tariff-driven and transitory; the AI infra cycle is intact."

▸ Filtered News · What actually moved tape
MACRO · INFLATION

April PPI +6.0% YoY — biggest since December 2022

Headline PPI +1.4% MoM (consensus +0.5%, prior revised to +0.7%), +6.0% YoY (vs +5.0%) — largest monthly print since March 2022. Core PPI +1.0% MoM (vs +0.4%), +2.8% YoY. Services +1.2% with trade services +2.7% driving two-thirds of the move — a signal that tariff costs are beginning to flow through to prices. Fed funds futures: rate-cut odds for the rest of 2026 ≈ 0%; cut odds collapsed and hike odds climbed to about 39%. 5/14 retail sales and import prices are the next inflection.

Source: CNBC · Bloomberg · BLS · Babypips (2026-05-13)
COMPANY · AI INFRA

Cisco beats + AI infra order target raised $5B → $9B (+80%)

Q3 EPS $1.06 vs $1.00 est, revenue $15.84B vs $15.56B est — both beats. The key was guidance: FY26 AI infrastructure order target raised from $5B to $9B (+80%). FY26 revenue guided to $62.8–63.0B (above prior); EPS to $4.27–4.29 (above prior $4.13–4.17). Third consecutive quarterly guide-up — strengthens consensus confidence. Shares +14–15% in after-hours (CNBC: "sharpest rally since 2011" if it sticks). Setup: networking / AI-infra peer gap-ups likely into 5/14 — monitor JNPR, ANET, NTAP, CIEN for sympathy.

Source: CNBC · TheStreet · 24/7 Wall St · Cisco IR (2026-05-13)
COMPANY · AUTOS

Ford +13% — Morgan Stanley flags "energy storage as hyperscaler card"

Ford Motor +13% to $13.56 — biggest single-day move since March 2020. Core of the Morgan Stanley bull call: "Ford's energy-storage business could lead to a hyperscaler deal". Q1 beat + raised FY guide + a 3-pronged EV / software / energy roadmap layered on top. Stacks up well vs peers: GM +0.4%, Stellantis +2.7%, TSLA +3.9%. Top S&P 500 contributor on the day.

Source: Bloomberg · Motley Fool · 24/7 Wall St · Invezz (2026-05-13)
COMPANY · EARNINGS MISS

Wix -30% — Q1 miss + margin collapse is the real story

Revenue $541.2M (+14% YoY) vs $544.2M est — a modest top-line miss. The bigger problem is margins: adj. gross margin 69% → 66%, adj. operating margin 21% → 5% (a 15-point drop). Adj. EPS $0.68 vs $1.25 est — nearly cut in half. Full-year guidance was reaffirmed, but the market priced concern about structural margin pressure from AI-search-driven traffic shifts on SMB SaaS economics. Watch SHOP, WEAVE, SQSP for sympathy moves into 5/14.

Source: Benzinga · Seeking Alpha · Motley Fool · Investing.com (2026-05-13)
MACRO · RATES · OIL

10Y at new YTD high + WTI cools to ~$102 after 3-day rally

The PPI print pushed the 10-year yield to a new YTD high (above 5/12's 4.46%) — adding pressure on duration-heavy SW, REITs, and utilities. WTI hovered near $102 — a small pullback after 3 days of roughly +7% gains. IEA reported global crude inventories fell at a record ~4 mb/d pace in March and April, keeping the fundamental floor. Fed Chair Powell's term ends Friday 5/15 — succession and policy-leadership uncertainty is part of the bond-selling backdrop.

Source: CNBC · Bloomberg · Reuters · Trading Economics (2026-05-13)

BOTTOM LINE · Setup into 5/14

The takeaway from 5/13 is simple: "Even when inflation prints hot, the AI infrastructure cycle is intact" — and that bet won the day. Despite a clearly hot PPI (+6.0% YoY / +1.4% MoM), the S&P and Nasdaq still closed at all-time highs, and Cisco's after-hours +15% (AI infra order target +80%) added decisive fuel to that bet.

Setup into 5/14: networking / AI infrastructure (Cisco sympathy) + semiconductor equipment (AMAT expectation) is the cleanest long lean. Ford +13% extends the EV / energy-storage chase. Duration-heavy / rate-sensitive names (utilities, REITs) face a higher 10Y headwind.

Traps to avoid: the Wix pattern — SaaS earnings miss + margin compression — is a short-term avoid signal for SMB SaaS broadly. Don't bottom-fish Coterra-style misses or CEG-style rate-sensitive unwinds yet.

Key variable: 5/14 US retail sales + Walmart guide. If both hold up, the "PPI is transitory" narrative hardens — fresh highs available. If both come in soft, the "tariff filtering through" narrative gets traction, and the 10Y-above-4.5% pressure broadens into more duration names.
