Today's Verdict

Mainland A-shares and Hong Kong stocks moved in opposite directions as policy divergence split investor flows. CSI 300 climbed 1.59% to 5,020.1 while Shenzhen Component surged 1.82% to 16,344.08, fueled by NDRC battery subsidy expansion and PBOC green finance quota increases targeting renewable energy. Hang Seng dropped 1.45% to 23,072 and HSCEI fell 2.04% to 7,606.24 as platform fee scrutiny headlines pressured Tencent, Meituan, and Xiaomi. Northbound capital flipped to net buying after three consecutive sessions of outflows, pouring 28 billion yuan into CATL and 22 billion into BYD. The policy-driven Mainland rally contrasts sharply with Hong Kong's regulatory overhang, setting up a short-term bifurcation in China equity flows.

📊 Policy Catalysts Drive Mainland Divergence from Hong Kong

The NDRC battery subsidy expansion and PBOC green finance quota increase created a powerful Mainland rally centered on EV, battery, and renewable energy plays. This policy momentum pushed CSI 300 above 5,020 while leaving Hong Kong stocks behind under regulatory pressure.

NDRC Battery Subsidy Expansion Ignites EV & Battery Sector

CATL surged 4.8% to ¥432.50 on 28 billion yuan of northbound buying after NDRC announced expanded battery manufacturing subsidies targeting domestic capacity for EVs and energy storage. BYD gained 3.5% to ¥298.20 supported by June NEV sales beating expectations at 420,000 units and 22 billion yuan of foreign inflow. Cambricon Technologies rallied 3.2% to ¥187.60 on confirmation of AI chip orders from Alibaba Cloud, pulling 12 billion yuan of northbound capital. The EV & Battery theme gained 3.2% overall, the session's strongest sector performance.

PBOC Green Finance Quota Lifts Renewable Energy Complex

Longi Green Energy climbed 2.9% to ¥34.80 after PBOC raised green finance lending quota by 15% for renewable energy projects, drawing 15 billion yuan of northbound buying. Solar and wind stocks rallied on easier financing access expectations. The policy support created a clear rotation into green infrastructure plays, contrasting with weakness in traditional consumer discretionary and internet platform names.

Hong Kong Tech Selloff on Platform Fee Scrutiny

Meituan dropped 2.58% to HK$66.0 and Xiaomi fell 3.05% to HK$22.26 as State Administration for Market Regulation initiated review of e-commerce and delivery platform fee structures for merchant fee transparency. Tencent slid 1.40% to HK$422.8 on gaming revenue headwinds from new minor protection rules. Alibaba ADR fell 2.73% to $99.8 on cloud revenue guidance cuts. Southbound capital sold 11 billion yuan of Tencent, 9 billion of Meituan, and 7 billion of Xiaomi, driving Hang Seng Tech down 2.27% to 5,182.34. The regulatory headline risk created a sharp divergence from Mainland policy optimism.
