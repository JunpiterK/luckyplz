Why SMIC / NAURA / AMEC Are in the Crosshairs

SMIC generates over 60% of revenue from advanced packaging and legacy-node production for Chinese fabless designers. When US equipment orders fall, it signals softer global demand and tighter export-control enforcement—both headwinds for SMIC's capex cycle. NAURA (North Hua Chuang) and AMEC (Advanced Micro-Fabrication Equipment) are pure capital-equipment plays; their order books are directly tied to Mainland fab spending. Yesterday's State Council subsidy announcement (see CN News section) may provide a 1-2% cushion, but it won't fully offset a -5.71% SOX rout. Watch ¥72.0 support on SMIC A-share; a break below that level opens ¥68-69 (early June lows).
