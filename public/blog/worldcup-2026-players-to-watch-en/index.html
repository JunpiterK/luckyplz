🏆 World Cup 2026 · Players to Watch

Between the Last Dance and the First Stage, 10 Players

Some are preparing to say goodbye; others, to make their debut. These are the 10 players who, on talent and sheer star power, will pull the eyes of the planet toward the 2026 North American World Cup. From 38-year-old Messi's last dance to 18-year-old Yamal's first, it's a stage where one generation crosses paths with the next.

Jun 11, 2026Reading time 8 minWORLD CUP 2026

The World Cup is great because, in the end, it's about people. Tactics and data ultimately converge on the 0.3-second magic that a single player conjures in front of goal. This edition is especially thick with that "human story." The stars who ruled an era are trying to shine their last, while the young stars angling for their place step onto the biggest stage for the very first time. We've assigned a ranking, but honestly, the numbers mean little to these ten. Every one of them is the hottest for a reason of his own.

01
🇦🇷 Lionel Messi
Argentina · Inter Miami · FW · 38
Last Dance · Defending Champion
Messi, who finally got his star in Qatar in 2022, takes the field at his sixth World Cup. The explosive peak may be gone, but his vision and his strike from the final 30 meters are still the best on Earth. The last story the defending champions' No. 10 has to tell — that alone is the single biggest narrative of this tournament.
02
🇪🇸 Lamine Yamal
Spain · Barcelona · WG · 18
Next-Gen Ruler · First World Cup
The driving force behind Spain's Euro 2024 triumph now makes his World Cup debut. Just 18, yet he reads the game like a veteran. His right-footed cut-in and through balls are already among the world's very best. Some are even talking about the youngest Golden Boot in history. His is the first hand reaching to grab the torch Messi is laying down.
03
🇫🇷 Kylian Mbappé
France · Real Madrid · FW · 27
Redemption · Record Hunt
Mbappé scored a hat-trick in the 2022 final and still lost the title on penalties. He has carried that ache for four years. Now, squarely in his prime, he sits just one goal shy of France's all-time scoring record (Olivier Giroud's 57). At the peak of both pace and finishing, if you had to name the single most frightening man, it's him.
04
🇳🇴 Erling Haaland
Norway · Manchester City · ST · 25
World Cup Debut · 28 Years of Waiting
The world's best goalscorer is, astonishingly, making his World Cup debut — because it's been 28 years since Norway last reached the finals. He topped the European qualifiers with 16 goals, hauling his golden generation up by himself. He's been drawn into the group of death (Group I), but there's still no obvious way to stop him in front of goal.
05
🇵🇹 Cristiano Ronaldo
Portugal · Al Nassr · FW · 41
A Sixth — and Final — Time
Forty-one. His sixth World Cup, and by any measure his last. His rival Messi has lifted the trophy, but for Ronaldo that one page remains blank. The determination to fill the final piece under manager Roberto Martínez — whatever the result, it's hot enough as the farewell to an era.
06
🇧🇷 Vinícius Jr
Brazil · Real Madrid · WG · 25
2024 FIFA Player of the Year
In 2022 he was stuck on the bench; now, as the 2024 FIFA Player of the Year, he's the heart of Brazil's attack. He tears defenses apart with explosive dribbling and clinical finishing. He's the surest card to quench the Seleção's thirst for "campeão." His left foot is Brazil's hope itself.
07
🏴 Jude Bellingham
England · Real Madrid · MF · 22
King of the Midfield
At 22, Bellingham is already in the conversation as one of the world's best midfielders. Defending, breaking forward, scoring — he does it all on his own. Alongside Kane, he's the core engine to end the 60-year hurt of the game's birthplace, England (no trophy since 1966). The next-gen superstar closest to the finished article.
08
🇰🇷 Son Heung-min
South Korea · LAFC · WG · 33
Icon of Asia · Fourth World Cup
One of the finest attackers Asia has ever produced. This is his fourth World Cup and, as captain, effectively his last grand stage. Leaving his Tottenham legend behind to chase a new challenge at LAFC, he remains South Korea's sharpest weapon. The moment one of his strikes lands in Group A, the door to the round of 16 swings open.
09
🏴 Harry Kane
England · Bayern Munich · ST · 32
A Second Shot at the Golden Boot
The man behind the 2018 Golden Boot (6 goals). Keeping up his bombardment of a scoring record at Bayern, he's chasing a first-ever second Golden Boot. The desperation not to let his thirst for club silverware bleed into a trophyless international career makes him even more dangerous inside the penalty box.
10
🇪🇬 Mohamed Salah
Egypt · Liverpool · FW · 33
The Egyptian King
Liverpool's living legend, the "Egyptian King." He has won everything at club level, but a major trophy with the national team still eludes him. This is his second World Cup overall, and perhaps the last challenge at the tail end of his prime. The pride of Africa rides on that one right foot.

📋 The Names Waiting in the Next Row

They didn't all fit in the top ten, but the passing of Pedri (Spain), the left foot of Arda Güler (Türkiye), the explosiveness of Antoine Semenyo (Ghana), now at Man City, and the creativity of Lee Kang-in (South Korea), who has stepped up a level at PSG, can each take over a match all on their own. The World Cup always turns an unexpected name into a star — the next one might be a face we don't even know yet.

A crossing point where one generation fades and another rises. While Messi and Ronaldo prepare their final goodbyes, Yamal and Bellingham aim to prove their time has come. And in between them, Son Heung-min looks to leave the one scene Korean football will remember the longest. Thirty-nine days from now, whose name will we be shouting?
