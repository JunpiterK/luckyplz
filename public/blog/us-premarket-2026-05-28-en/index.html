📊 Core Catalyst — Q1 GDP Second Estimate & Corporate Profits (08:30 ET)

Today's 08:30 ET release marks the Bureau of Economic Analysis's second look at Q1 2026 growth. The advance estimate delivered +2.0% annualized GDP growth (up from Q4's +0.5%), driven by investment, exports, and government spending. Market consensus expects no revision. However, the simultaneous release of Q1 corporate profits data carries equal—if not greater—weight. Corporate profit margins serve as a real-time inflation gauge: if firms successfully passed along higher input costs (oil, labor) without demand destruction, it signals persistent pricing power and reduces Fed urgency to cut rates. Conversely, margin compression would validate disinflation and support dovish pivot expectations.

Market Reaction Framework

GDP upside surprise (≥2.3%) + strong profit margins → Treasury yields spike, equities sell off on delayed rate cuts. The 10Y yield at 4.48% and VIX at 16.29 suggest markets are pricing a benign outcome, leaving room for volatility if the print deviates.
