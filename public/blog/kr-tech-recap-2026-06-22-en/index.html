Today's Verdict

The Korean market closed with a bifurcated script: semiconductors surged on AI optimism while secondary battery and mobility themes collapsed under earnings pressure. SK Hynix's 5.61% rally on HBM4 mass production advancement reports lifted KOSPI 0.69% despite relentless foreign selling of ₩842B. Foreigners dumped Hyundai Motor (₩195.6B) and LG Energy Solution (₩154.2B) while accumulating SK Hynix (₩284.7B), reflecting a sharp pivot from old-economy cyclicals to AI infrastructure plays. Institutions bought ₩523B, concentrating firepower in semiconductors. The KOSPI 200 outperformed with a 1.22% gain, driven entirely by top-tier large-caps, while mid-cap battery and auto suppliers bled. Today's session confirms the market is pricing a narrow leadership regime: AI semiconductor exposure rewarded, EV-linked plays punished.

📊 Core Theme — Foreign Exodus from Mobility & Battery, AI Semiconductor Accumulation

Foreigners executed their most concentrated sector rotation in three months, selling ₩842B net while drastically reallocating within that flow. The headline number masks a structural shift: foreign capital fled secondary battery and auto at historic pace, dumping ₩195.6B of Hyundai Motor and ₩154.2B of LG Energy Solution in a single session, while simultaneously accumulating ₩284.7B of SK Hynix. This is not broad risk-off; it is surgical thematic repositioning toward AI memory infrastructure and away from EV-exposed names ahead of weak 2Q earnings.

SK Hynix HBM4 Catalyst — The AI Memory Arms Race Accelerates

SK Hynix surged 5.61% to ₩2,919,000 after reports emerged that the company advanced its HBM4 mass production schedule by six months to H2 2026, responding to surging AI server demand from hyperscalers. The stock accounted for roughly 45% of KOSPI's gains today, with foreigners and institutions combined buying ₩428B. The move signals market confidence that SK Hynix will capture expanded share in the next-generation HBM market, where supply constraints remain acute and pricing power is intact. Samsung Electronics traded flat at ₩353,500 despite the HBM tailwind, weighed by lingering foundry yield concerns.

Battery & Auto Earnings Warnings Trigger Capitulation

The mobility ecosystem suffered a rout. Hyundai Motor plunged 5.22% on brokerage downgrades forecasting 2Q operating profit 5-8% below consensus, citing rising North America incentive burdens and raw material cost pressures. LG Energy Solution dropped 4.70% as weak GM and Ford EV sales data deepened concerns over 2Q volume and margin. Samsung SDI fell 3.96%, EcoPro BM slid 1.59%, and POSCO Future M declined 3.76%, forming a correlated selloff across the battery supply chain. Foreigners sold the entire complex aggressively, erasing three weeks of inflows in a single day. The sector's underperformance reflects deteriorating North America EV demand visibility and fading IRA subsidy optimism.

Bottom Line

KOSPI's 0.69% gain is a Potemkin rally — strip out SK Hynix and the index would have closed flat or negative. The market is bifurcating into AI winners and EV losers, with foreign capital leading the charge. Semiconductors, particularly HBM plays, remain the only defensible chase in the current tape, while battery and auto face a multi-quarter de-rating cycle as North America EV demand craters. Institutions' ₩523B net buy signals domestic capital is aligned with the thematic pivot. For Tuesday, watch whether SK Hynix can hold ₩2.9M or if profit-taking emerges. Any further weakness in Tesla or GM EV sales data will deepen the battery sector rout. The risk now is that foreign selling broadens beyond mobility into platform and bio if global risk appetite deteriorates. Defensively positioned into semiconductors, avoid secondary battery and auto until 2Q earnings clarify the demand floor.
