Today's Verdict

China equities delivered a decisive rally on July 1 as CSI 300 surged +1.86% to 4,958.98, driven by a potent combination of policy stimulus expectations and semiconductor-led tech sector strength. The National Development and Reform Commission's ¥800 billion infrastructure package and the PBOC's 50bp reserve requirement ratio cut injected fresh optimism into a market that had been struggling with capital outflows. SMIC's +5.42% jump in Hong Kong on semiconductor supply chain headlines and Tencent's +2.28% gain on strong gaming and AI momentum underscored the rotation into growth names. Northbound capital reversed three consecutive days of net selling, pouring ¥32.5 billion into Tencent and ¥18.2 billion into SMIC. While Hang Seng lagged at -0.63% on profit-taking in financials and property, the mainland bourses showed broad-based strength with turnover exceeding ¥1.1 trillion, signaling renewed institutional conviction.

📊 Today's Defining Theme — Policy Stimulus Reignites Growth Appetite

The dominant narrative on July 1 was the sudden resurgence of policy support, which catalyzed a sharp rotation from defensive positioning into cyclical and tech exposure. The NDRC's ¥800 billion infrastructure package, targeting rail, water, and urban transit, combined with the PBOC's 50bp RRR cut releasing ¥1 trillion in liquidity, marked the most aggressive coordinated stimulus since Q1 2026. This dual policy punch addressed two persistent investor concerns: anemic fixed-asset investment growth and tight interbank liquidity. The immediate market reaction was a CSI 300 surge of +1.86%, with semiconductor, internet, and consumer discretionary sectors leading gains. The infrastructure announcement lifted construction materials and machinery names, while the RRR cut boosted banks and brokerages on expectations of credit expansion.

Semiconductor Surge — SMIC +5.42% Leads Chip Rally

SMIC's +5.42% rally to HK$89.40 anchored the semiconductor sector's outperformance, which posted a composite gain of +4.28%. Headlines around US-China chip cooperation and bullish analyst notes from Citi and HSBC raised price targets on foundry exposure. Northbound capital poured ¥18.2 billion into SMIC, the second-largest single-name inflow of the day. Hua Hong and NAURA also posted strong gains as investors bet on a bottoming of the semiconductor cycle and potential easing of export controls. The sector's strength was critical in lifting the broader CSI 300, as semiconductors account for 8.3% of the index weight. The rally also triggered a southbound flow of ¥15.3 billion into HK-listed chip names, reflecting mainland retail's chase for tech upside.

Northbound Reversal — ¥32.5B into Tencent After 3-Day Exodus

Northbound capital reversed course after three consecutive days of net outflows, injecting ¥32.5 billion into Tencent alone. The gaming giant's +2.28% gain to HK$429.80 was fueled by Q2 revenue guidance that beat consensus and raised AI monetization expectations. Meituan attracted ¥14.7 billion on Dragon Boat Festival delivery data showing +18% YoY GMV growth and drone delivery expansion to five new cities. The northbound reversal was broad-based, with WuXi Biologics and ICBC also drawing substantial inflows as investors rotated into both growth and defensive sectors. The shift marked a tactical capitulation by foreign institutions, who had been net sellers for much of June on macro uncertainty. Today's inflows suggest a reassessment of China's policy commitment and a tactical re-entry into quality names at compressed valuations.
