Today's Verdict

Mainland China equities delivered a decisive rally Tuesday, with the CSI 300 jumping 3.22% to 4,931.39, the biggest single-day gain in three months. The PBOC's ¥120 billion MLF injection at unchanged 2.50% rates, combined with the NDRC's expanded home appliance subsidy announcement, triggered aggressive buying in financials and consumer staples. Northbound Stock Connect channeled ¥73.7 billion net into A-shares, concentrated in banks (Ping An +5.82%, CMB +4.91%) and baijiu (Moutai +3.74% on strong May sales). Hong Kong markets went the other direction: the Hang Seng fell 0.86% to 24,282.97 and HSCEI dropped 1.31%, weighed by profit-taking in Meituan, BYD, and Xiaomi after recent outperformance. Southbound flows turned negative at -¥8.9 billion. The split reflects Mainland policy optimism versus Hong Kong's valuation caution and regulatory overhang.

📊 Today's Defining Theme — Policy Catalyst Ignites A-Share Financials & Consumer Rally

Today's session was dominated by a coordinated policy push that reignited the Mainland A-share bull case. The PBOC's MLF operation, while holding rates steady, injected critical liquidity into the banking system, signaling Beijing's commitment to maintain accommodative conditions through mid-year. The NDRC subsidy expansion—covering more appliance models and raising per-unit payments—provided a direct boost to consumer durables (Midea +2.77%) and reinforced the consumption-stimulus narrative. Northbound investors pounced, adding ¥28.4 billion to Ping An alone and ¥19.7 billion to China Merchants Bank, both names levered to easing financial conditions and improving net interest margin outlooks.

Financial Sector Leadership — Banks & Insurers Surge on Easing Signal

The financial sector led the CSI 300 rally with a 4.15% sector gain. Ping An Insurance jumped 5.82% after the PBOC move, China Merchants Bank climbed 4.91% on upgraded margin guidance tied to loan-demand recovery, and ICBC rose 3.01% on rumors of expanded state-bank lending quotas. Brokerages also participated: CITIC Securities gained 2.89% as IPO pipeline reacceleration lifted sentiment. Northbound flows into financials totaled over ¥70 billion, the heaviest single-day concentration this quarter.

Consumer Staples Shine — Moutai Rally on Strong May Data

Baijiu stocks delivered standout performance. Kweichow Moutai advanced 3.74% after disclosing May wholesale volume growth of 8.2% YoY, reaffirming premium spirits demand resilience despite macro headwinds. Wuliangye followed with +3.21%. The consumer staples sector rose 2.87%, with northbound adding ¥16.2 billion to Moutai and ¥12.5 billion to Wuliangye. The NDRC subsidy news extended gains to appliance makers, with Midea and Gree both closing near session highs. This rotation into defensive consumption and financials suggests investors are positioning for policy-driven stability rather than growth acceleration.

Hong Kong Divergence & Cross-Border Flow Split

Hong Kong's underperformance versus Mainland was stark. The Hang Seng fell 0.86% and HSCEI dropped 1.31%, with losses concentrated in large-cap tech and property. Meituan slid 1.53% on renewed antitrust investigation extension headlines, BYD declined 2.50% after May NEV sales growth decelerated sequentially, and Xiaomi dropped 1.48% on smartphone inventory buildup concerns flagged in channel checks. Tencent edged down 0.36% after a sell-side analyst trimmed gaming revenue estimates. Only SMIC bucked the trend, rising 2.30% on domestic semiconductor self-sufficiency buying. Southbound Stock Connect recorded net selling of ¥8.9 billion, led by exits from Meituan (-¥6.3B), BYD (-¥5.1B), and Tencent (-¥4.6B). The flow pattern underscores a Mainland preference for policy-levered domestic plays and a Hong Kong preference to de-risk ahead of earnings uncertainty.
