Today's Verdict

Tuesday's session delivered a textbook risk-off rotation as technology stocks crumbled under the weight of rising rates and surging oil prices. The S&P 500 fell 1.62% to 7266.99 while the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.98%, with semiconductors bearing the brunt of the selloff as the SOX index plunged 3.57%. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed 0.31% to 4.54%, its highest level in months, while WTI crude surged 3.04% to $90.88 on supply concerns. The VIX spiked 12.23% to 22.3, signaling heightened anxiety. Defensive sectors outperformed sharply, with Consumer Staples rising 1.65% and Energy gaining 1.50%, underscoring a clear flight to safety. For Korean markets opening Wednesday, semiconductor exposure and rate-sensitive growth names face immediate pressure, while energy and defensive plays may find support.

📊 Core Theme — Oil Surge and Rate Spike Squeeze Growth Stocks

The session's defining narrative was a double squeeze on high-valuation growth stocks: surging oil prices reignited inflation fears just as Treasury yields climbed to multi-month highs. WTI crude's 3.04% jump to $90.88 reflected tightening supply conditions, lifting energy stocks but adding inflationary pressure that pushed the 10-year yield up 0.31% to 4.54%. Technology stocks, particularly semiconductors, bore the brunt as the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index plunged 3.57%, with AVGO down 5.12% and NVDA falling 3.73%. The VIX surged 12.23% to 22.3, its highest close since early May, as investors rotated aggressively into defensive sectors.

Rate-Driven Tech Selloff Deepens

The 10-year Treasury yield's climb to 4.54% marked its highest level in over three months, pressuring high-multiple technology stocks across the board. The Technology sector ETF (XLK) fell 2.29%, while the Nasdaq's 1.98% decline outpaced the broader market. Industrials also suffered, with XLI dropping 3.41% as economic slowdown concerns weighed on cyclical names. Apple was the sole Mag 7 gainer at +0.35%, reflecting defensive rotation within mega-caps.

Energy Outperforms as Defensives Rally

Energy stocks surged on WTI's jump above $90, with XLE rising 1.50%. ExxonMobil gained 3.42%, Chevron added 3.18%, and ConocoPhillips climbed 2.95% as refining margins and upstream profitability outlooks improved. Consumer Staples led defensive sectors with a 1.65% gain, as Procter & Gamble rose 2.21%, Coca-Cola added 1.87%, and PepsiCo climbed 1.76%. Utilities and Real Estate held flat, providing relative stability amid the broader selloff.

Implications for Korean Markets

Korean semiconductor exporters face immediate headwinds from the SOX index's 3.57% plunge, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix likely to track Tuesday's US chip weakness. The won's 0.55% strengthening to 1520.54 per dollar offers modest relief but won't offset global sector pressure. Energy-linked names like SK Innovation and S-Oil may find support from WTI's surge, while defensive plays in Consumer Staples and Utilities could attract safe-haven flows. Rate-sensitive growth stocks in IT services and secondary batteries face downside risk as US yields climb.
