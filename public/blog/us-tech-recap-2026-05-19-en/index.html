Treasury Yield Shock — 4.58% Break Ripples Through Markets

The 10-year Treasury yield surged 8 basis points to 4.58%, the highest level since March 2025, while the 2-year hit 4.12% and the 30-year reached 4.89%. The 2s10s curve steepened sharply to 46bp, signaling easing recession fears as growth expectations firmed. Persistent hawkish Fed rhetoric drove the move, with markets pricing lower odds of rate cuts in 2026. Tech and growth stocks bore the brunt of valuation pressure from higher discount rates, with Nasdaq underperforming the S&P 500 by 36 basis points due to duration sensitivity.

Semiconductor Selloff — AI Valuation Doubts Resurface

NVIDIA plunged 2.8% to $118.45 and semiconductor ETF SMH fell 2.3% as AI valuation concerns resurfaced. Broadcom dropped 2.44%, AMD fell 2.1%, and Intel declined 1.8% in broad chip weakness. Goldman Sachs cited uncertainty around AI infrastructure investment returns and recommended reducing semiconductor exposure, triggering a sharp sentiment shift. Peak AI chip demand fears combined with stretched valuations sparked selling across the tech sector, with XLK technology falling 1.82%.

Implications for Korean Markets — Chip Weakness vs. Won Weakness

The US semiconductor selloff poses direct pressure on Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, particularly as AI valuation reassessment raises concerns about HBM premium compression. However, USD/KRW rising 5.8 won to 1,382.5 provides a partial offset for exporters, with traditional export sectors like shipbuilding, chemicals, and steel likely showing relative resilience. The surge in US yields ahead of the BOK rate decision (May 22) may constrain Korean monetary policy flexibility, and KOSPI support at the 2,700 level will be a near-term pivot point.
