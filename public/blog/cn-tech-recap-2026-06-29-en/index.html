Today's Verdict

China's equity markets delivered a decisive rally on June 29, with the CSI 300 advancing 1.21% to 4,926.92 and Hang Seng jumping 1.48% to 23,007.57. Northbound flows reversed to net inflows of approximately 42 billion yuan after three consecutive sessions of outflows, concentrated in tech platforms and semiconductors. The Hang Seng Tech index surged 2.87%, outpacing the broader market, as Tencent, Meituan, and SMIC led gains on sector-specific catalysts. Mainland turnover remained elevated near 1.2 trillion yuan, reflecting sustained participation. The session's strength was rooted in policy support (PBOC liquidity injection, MIIT AI chip roadmap) and micro catalysts (Meituan guidance upgrade, SMIC capacity expansion), signaling renewed risk appetite for China exposure after weeks of cautious positioning.

📊 Today's Defining Theme — Northbound Flow Reversal and Tech Platform Leadership

The reversal of northbound flows from three days of outflows to a net inflow of approximately 42 billion yuan marked the session's pivotal narrative. This shift was heavily concentrated in Hang Seng-listed platforms and A-share semiconductors, reflecting a tactical rotation back into growth and technology.

Northbound accumulation pattern: Tencent and Meituan as anchors

Tencent drew 18.5 billion yuan in northbound inflows, the largest single-name allocation, as WeChat Pay merchant transaction volume accelerated in June and gaming revenue channel checks beat expectations. Meituan attracted 12.3 billion yuan following a brokerage upgrade tied to Q2 order growth guidance revision and delivery margin expansion. SMIC captured 9.8 billion yuan on confirmation of 7nm foundry capacity expansion and reports of domestic AI chip order backlogs. These three names alone accounted for over 40 billion yuan of the day's northbound activity, underscoring the thematic concentration in internet platforms and semiconductor infrastructure.

Semiconductor strength: MIIT roadmap and capacity expansion

The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's announcement of a three-year AI chip self-sufficiency roadmap, targeting 70% domestic penetration by 2028, provided a macro tailwind for the semiconductor complex. SMIC rose 5.62% to HK$84.5, with northbound and southbound flows converging on the 7nm capacity expansion narrative and elevated AI chip order visibility. Naura and Hua Hong Semi also rallied, though specific northbound data for these names was not independently verified. The sector's outperformance was reinforced by policy clarity and domestic demand visibility, offsetting prior concerns over US export restrictions and lithium-carbonate-linked margin pressure in adjacent battery and solar segments.

Platform rebound: Meituan's margin expansion and Xiaomi's EV momentum

Meituan's 5.21% surge to HK$67.6 was the session's standout platform move, driven by a brokerage upgrade citing Q2 order growth acceleration and delivery margin expansion. Southbound inflows into Meituan reached 15.7 billion yuan, signaling mainland institutional conviction. Xiaomi rose 1.68% to HK$21.78 on reports that its SU7 EV waiting list extended to 10 weeks, with production ramp-up ahead of schedule. Northbound flows into Xiaomi totaled 6.7 billion yuan, reflecting optimism around the EV vertical's contribution to the hardware ecosystem. These micro catalysts provided stock-specific drivers that complemented the broader tech platform recovery narrative.

Bottom Line

Today's session represented a clear shift from the prior week's defensive positioning to renewed risk-on appetite in China equities. The northbound flow reversal and concentration in Tencent, Meituan, and SMIC suggest tactical conviction in platforms and semiconductors, supported by both policy (MIIT AI chip roadmap, PBOC liquidity) and micro catalysts (Meituan guidance, SMIC capacity). The Hang Seng Tech index's 2.87% outperformance and Hang Seng China Enterprises' 1.89% gain underscore the rotation back into mainland exposure. Near-term risks include US-China export control headlines, lithium price pressure on EV supply chains, and quarter-end volatility. However, the combination of stabilizing PMI preliminary data (50.2, first above 50 in three months) and sector-specific earnings revisions provides a constructive setup into July. Watch for sustained northbound inflows above 30 billion yuan daily and Hang Seng Tech holding above 4,800 as confirmation signals for continued upside momentum.
