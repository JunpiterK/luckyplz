8 Countries' Lotteries Compared — Which One Is Most Your Style?

2026-04-19 · 7 min read · 🎱 Lottery

Not all lotteries are created equal. The number of balls you draw and the range they come from change from country to country, so winning odds can differ by hundreds of times. Lucky Blog has lotteries from 16 countries, and today we're picking 8 of the big ones for a tour of "which country's lottery is the most your style" 🎱.

🇰🇷Korea Lotto 6/45

Range 1–45 Main 6 balls Bonus same pool Jackpot odds 1 in 8.14M

The rules we all know best. From 45 balls, you draw 6 + 1 bonus (same pool) = 6+1. The prize structure runs deep across tiers 1 through 5, and the key point is that matching just 3 lands you a tier-5 prize. There's a strong "let's win something, anything" vibe.

It's also a national favorite for friendly group raffles. A common twist: in a group of 7, you draw numbers, and if yours comes up you're the one buying ☕.

🇺🇸US Powerball

Range 1–69 + 1–26 Main 5 balls Powerball separate pool Jackpot odds 1 in 290M

The 5 main numbers run from 1–69, and the Powerball is drawn from an entirely separate pool (1–26) — a two-pool structure. The jackpot odds are roughly 35 times lower than Korea's, but in exchange rollovers let the jackpot pile up into the billions of dollars. It's the one you see in the news as a "trillion-won jackpot."

In a word, it's the all-in style. For small-stakes fun, Korea's lottery is better; Powerball is for the "one big dream" play.

🇺🇸US Mega Millions

Range 1–70 + 1–25 Main 5 balls Mega Ball separate pool Jackpot odds 1 in 300M

Practically a twin of Powerball. Only the ranges differ slightly (69→70, 26→25). The actual odds are similar, but the all-time record jackpot is a touch bigger here. Run both and pick whichever one pulls you in 🎰.

🇨🇳China Shuangseqiu (双色球, "Two-Color Ball")

Range 1–33 + 1–16 Main (red) 6 balls Blue separate pool, 1 ball Jackpot odds 1 in 17.7M

Just as the name suggests, it's a "two-color" structure of 6 red balls + 1 blue ball. Difficulty sits between Korea and Powerball. Drawing red and blue separately makes the odds about twice as tight as Korea's — landing tiers 3 and 4 is harder than in Korea.

🇯🇵Japan Loto 6, Loto 7 & Mini Loto

Range varies Bonus same pool

Japan runs three lotteries side by side. Pick to taste:

  • ロト6 (Loto 6): 6+1 from 1–43 — practically a twin of Korea's
  • ロト7 (Loto 7): 7+2 from 1–37 — with 2 bonus balls, the tier combinations are more varied
  • ミニロト (Mini Loto): 5+1 from 1–31 — jackpot odds of 1 in 170K. Ridiculously easy 🎯

All of them draw main + bonus from the same pool (Korean style). Mini Loto is the best for getting a feel for it when "I've never played the lottery."

🇪🇺Europe EuroMillions

Range 1–50 + 1–12 Main 5 balls Lucky Star separate pool, 2 balls Jackpot odds 1 in 140M

A lottery played jointly by 9 countries, including France, the UK, and Spain. With 5 main + 2 Lucky Stars, the combinations are fairly complex. The odds are twice as good as Powerball, so Europeans tend to play this one a lot instead of the US jackpots ✨.

🇬🇧UK Lotto

Range 1–59 Main 6 balls Bonus same pool Jackpot odds 1 in 45M

Britain's traditional homegrown lottery. With 59 balls, the wide range makes it tight. The draw method is the same as Korea's, but the range difference pushes the difficulty up about fivefold. The type that sits quietly behind EuroMillions 🇬🇧.

🇧🇷Brazil Mega-Sena

Range 1–60 Main 6 balls Bonus none Jackpot odds 1 in 50M

Brazil's flagship lottery. A minimalist structure that draws just 6 numbers, with no bonus. With a range of 60 the odds are tight, but the rules are the simplest of the bunch, so there's nothing to explain 🇧🇷.
