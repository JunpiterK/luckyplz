📊 Policy-Driven Rally Splits Mainland from Hong Kong Tech

The dominant narrative today is the stark contrast between Mainland policy-fueled strength and Hong Kong's tech weakness. NDRC's consumption stimulus announcement, targeting durable goods subsidies and service sector support, ignited buying across A-share financials and consumer names, lifting CSI 300 by its largest single-day gain in weeks. Baijiu leaders Kweichow Moutai +4.82% and Wuliangye +4.12% surged on consumption policy expectations, while banks rallied on credit easing signals with Ping An Insurance +4.35% and China Merchants Bank +3.95% leading financials higher.

Northbound Flows Reinforce Policy Trade

Northbound investors committed ¥58.6B net today, the strongest single-day inflow in over a month. Kweichow Moutai attracted +¥28.4B, Ping An Insurance +¥18.2B, and China Merchants Bank +¥15.6B, reflecting concentrated positioning in policy-sensitive consumer and financial plays. In contrast, CATL saw -¥8.6B net selling and BYD -¥6.2B as investors rotated away from EV names despite the broader Mainland rally, signaling concerns over intensifying competition and margin pressure in the electric vehicle sector.

Hong Kong Tech Tumbles on Profit-Taking

Hong Kong markets diverged sharply as tech stocks faced heavy selling. Hang Seng fell -2.15% and Hang Seng China Enterprises dropped -2.61%, with Meituan down -4.10% on profit-taking after recent gains and Xiaomi losing -3.70% on EV competition worries. Southbound flows turned negative with Meituan suffering -¥9.2B net selling and Xiaomi -¥7.4B, underscoring Mainland investors' shift away from HK tech.

Policy Catalysts Drive Sector Rotation

The PBOC's continued liquidity support stance, paired with NDRC's consumption-focused measures, triggered sector rotation into financials, consumer staples, and infrastructure. Banking names rallied across the board, with the sector up over 4% on average, while semiconductors showed mild strength with SMIC edging +0.26% higher in Hong Kong. Healthcare rebounded with Jiangsu Hengrui Medicine +3.78% as investors priced in potential policy support for the sector. The divergence between Mainland policy optimism and HK tech caution highlights the challenge facing Chinese equities: domestic stimulus fuels cyclical strength, but structural concerns over tech regulation and EV competition keep growth stocks under pressure.
