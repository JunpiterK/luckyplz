Today's Verdict

June 4 delivered a textbook defensive rotation. The Dow surged 1.73% and the Russell 2000 gained 1.45% as investors fled semiconductors for financials, healthcare, and real estate following a weaker-than-expected ISM Services PMI. Broadcom's AI revenue guidance miss triggered a 12.59% collapse, dragging the SOX index down 2.15% and keeping Nasdaq barely negative at -0.09%. Treasury yields fell across the curve (10Y -0.31% to 4.48%), signaling easing rate-hike fears, while VIX dropped 4.42% to 15.35. The split reflects a market pricing in slower growth but not recession, favoring value and cyclicals over high-multiple tech.

📊 Today's Core Theme — Broadcom Shock Meets Defensive Rotation

The session's defining story was the stark divergence between old-economy cyclicals and semiconductor-dependent tech. Broadcom's FQ2 earnings report revealed AI chip revenue guidance below consensus, attributed to Blackwell transition delays and customer inventory adjustments. The stock's 12.59% plunge sent tremors through the semiconductor complex: KLAC -4.12%, LRCX -3.87%, AMAT -3.64%, QCOM -3.22%. Even NVDA, which closed +1.82%, faced pressure in after-hours trading as investors questioned the sustainability of AI infrastructure spending. The XLK Technology ETF fell 1.58%, the worst performer among 11 GICS sectors.

ISM Services Miss Triggers Rotation

The May ISM Services PMI printed 50.8 versus 52.3 expected, with new orders and employment sub-indices both weakening. Markets interpreted this as evidence of cooling demand, not imminent recession, and rotated capital into sectors that benefit from lower rates and stable growth. XLF Financials surged 2.63% (JPM +2.89%, GS +2.52%), XLV Health Care gained 3.07% (UNH +3.45%, LLY +2.76%), and XLRE Real Estate rose 2.05%. The 10-year Treasury yield fell 0.31% to 4.48%, and the 2s10s curve steepened slightly, signaling reduced near-term rate-hike expectations.
