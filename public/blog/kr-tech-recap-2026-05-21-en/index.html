Today's Verdict

KOSPI surged 8.42% to close at 7,815.59, posting the largest single-day gain since the March 2020 COVID crash. Reuters reported 30 minutes before market open that the White House and China's Commerce Ministry had reached draft agreement on phased tariff removal and relaxed semiconductor export controls, triggering explosive buying in futures and cash markets. SK Hynix jumped 11.2% on HBM export normalization hopes, while Hyundai Motor rallied 12.5% on retreating North American tariff risks. Foreign investors reversed six straight days of selling with ₩1.24 trillion net buying on KOSPI, joined by institutions adding ₩480 billion. Turnover hit ₩32.8 trillion, the highest in six months, as panic buying pushed over 180 stocks to daily limit-up within minutes of opening.

📊 Core Theme — US-China Deal Sparks Semiconductor & Auto Export Surge

The singular catalyst driving today's market was the Reuters report on an imminent US-China tariff deal. According to the pre-market report, the White House and China's Commerce Ministry agreed on a draft framework for phased mutual tariff elimination over six months and substantial easing of export controls on HBM, AI chips, and EV battery materials. Korean export stocks surged across the board, with semiconductor, battery, and auto sectors recording double-digit gains as investors priced in normalized China trade and stabilized global supply chains.

Foreign Flow — ₩1.2tn Net Buy Reverses 6-Day Sell Streak

Foreign investors bought a net ₩1.24 trillion on KOSPI, abruptly reversing six consecutive days of selling that had totaled -₩2.8 trillion. SK Hynix (+₩482bn) and Samsung Electronics (+₩315bn) topped the buy list as HBM export normalization hopes drove concentrated inflows. Hyundai Motor (+₩168bn), LG Energy Solution (+₩92bn), and NAVER (+₩74bn) followed. Conversely, Hanwha Aerospace (-₩52bn) and Samsung Biologics (-₩38bn) faced profit-taking pressure in defense and biotech sectors. On KOSDAQ, foreigners added ₩320 billion net, focusing on mid-cap exporters like EcoPro BM and Celltrion.

Semiconductor & Auto Rally — China Trade Normalization Benefits

SK Hynix surged 11.17% to ₩1.94 million and disclosed via KIND a ₩2 trillion HBM3E supply contract with a North American cloud provider. Samsung Electronics climbed 8.51% to reclaim ₩299,500. Auto sector saw Hyundai Motor jump 12.5% to a new all-time high of ₩666,000, while Kia gained 10.8%. Battery theme stocks rallied in tandem: EcoPro BM +10.4%, LG Energy Solution +4.3%, Samsung SDI +8.1%, reflecting expectations for resumed cathode and separator exports to China. Shipbuilding and defense stocks lagged, with Hanwha Aerospace flat at -0.08% and HD Hyundai Heavy up a modest 5.7%.

Policy Momentum — K-Chips Act Extended 3 Years, Tax Credit Raised to 20%

Korea's Ministry of Finance announced today an amendment extending semiconductor and battery capex tax credits through 2029 and raising the large-cap credit rate from 15% to 20%. The enhanced tax support for mega-projects such as Samsung's Pyeongtaek/Yongin foundry expansions and SK Hynix's Yongin M15X HBM fab strengthened long-term investment appeal. Markets view the policy move, coinciding with the US-China trade breakthrough, as a significant boost to Korea's global semiconductor competitiveness and a catalyst for sustained capex momentum through the end of the decade.
