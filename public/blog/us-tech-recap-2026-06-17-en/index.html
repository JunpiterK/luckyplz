Today's Verdict

Tuesday delivered a sharp repricing across US equities as the S&P 500 shed 1.21% and the Nasdaq fell 1.34%, with Meta's 5.44% collapse leading Mag 7 losses on escalating concerns over digital advertising demand and mounting AI infrastructure costs. The selloff was broad-based, with all 11 GICS sectors closing red, led by communication services down 2.78% and consumer discretionary down 2.51%. Against this tide, Broadcom surged 4.30% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index gained 1.38% on reports of accelerating AI networking chip orders from hyperscalers. The VIX exploded 12.55% to 18.47, signaling a sudden shift to risk-off positioning as hedging flows intensified. Treasury yields diverged sharply, with the 10-year falling 0.53% to 4.46% on safe-haven demand while the 2-year rose 0.80% to 3.65%, flattening the curve and reflecting mixed signals on Fed policy. The dollar index strengthened 0.89% to 100.43 on global growth concerns. For Korean markets opening Wednesday, expect pronounced volatility with semiconductor stocks likely to track the SOX strength but broader indices under pressure from the Mag 7 selloff and rising won weakness at 1,528.22.

📊 Core Theme — Mega-Cap Divergence Meets Fear Spike

The defining narrative of Tuesday's session was the violent divergence within technology leadership, as Meta's advertising-driven collapse and Microsoft's cloud growth concerns clashed with Broadcom's AI infrastructure rally, triggering a sharp rotation that left the VIX surging and investors scrambling for safety. Meta's 5.44% plunge was the session's worst Mag 7 performance, driven by analyst warnings that digital advertising demand is decelerating faster than expected and AI infrastructure spending is eroding margins. Microsoft fell 3.79% on mounting concerns that Azure cloud growth is slowing and AI monetization is lagging ambitious targets. Amazon dropped 3.46% as consumer demand weakness persisted and AWS faced intensifying competition. This trio of losses, collectively representing over $300 billion in market cap erosion, overwhelmed the broader market and sent communication services down 2.78% and consumer discretionary down 2.51%.

Broadcom and SOX Buck the Trend

In stark contrast, Broadcom surged 4.30% on reports that hyperscalers are accelerating AI networking chip orders, with the company's custom ASIC and Ethernet switching solutions seeing unprecedented demand. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rallied 1.38%, driven by Broadcom and select AI infrastructure plays, even as Nvidia fell 1.33% and Apple declined 1.10%. This divergence underscores a critical inflection: investors are rotating from consumer-facing AI applications toward the infrastructure layer, betting that networking and custom silicon will deliver more predictable revenue streams as the AI capex cycle matures.

VIX Spike and Yield Curve Dynamics

The VIX's 12.55% surge to 18.47 marked the sharpest single-day spike in weeks, reflecting a sudden rush to hedge equity exposure amid deteriorating breadth and mounting uncertainty over Fed policy. Treasury yields told a bifurcated story: the 10-year fell 0.53% to 4.46% as investors fled to safety, while the 2-year rose 0.80% to 3.65% on hawkish Fed expectations, flattening the 2s10s curve to levels last seen during regional banking stress. The dollar index climbed 0.89% to 100.43, further pressuring risk assets and tightening financial conditions. Crude oil fell 1.05% to $75.25, while gold dropped 1.12% to $4,282.3 and silver tumbled 2.80% to $67.94, as neither safe-haven metals nor commodities found buyers in the broad risk-off move.

Implications for Korean Markets

For Korean traders, Wednesday's session will likely see sharp differentiation between semiconductor exporters and domestic demand plays. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix should track the SOX's 1.38% gain and Broadcom's rally, particularly given the AI networking chip theme. However, platform and advertising-exposed names may face pressure following Meta's collapse. The won's 0.99% weakening to 1,528.22 and the dollar's broad strength will weigh on import-heavy sectors while supporting export margins. Watch for heightened volatility as KOSPI futures digest the VIX spike and the Mag 7 divergence. Key resistance for KOSPI lies at 2,850, with support at 2,820 if the selloff accelerates overnight.
