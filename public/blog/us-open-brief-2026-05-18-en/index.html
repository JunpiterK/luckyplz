On the flip side, the May 15 OPEX unwind plus a single -1.2% session has set up technical bounce justification too. The key today: how much further the market prices in "Warsh hawkish" vs. an oversold bounce vs. fresh Iran headlines — a tug of war.

▸ Weekend Macro — What Matters Most for the Open
Macro · Fed

Kevin Warsh confirmed as 17th Fed Chair — hawkish pricing in motion

Senate vote May 13: 54-45 (closest in modern era). With a 2006–2011 hawkish FOMC voting record, and amid accelerating inflation, oil above $100, and shelter inflation that doubled in April, a "hold or even hike" scenario is being floated despite Trump's preference for cuts. The market keeps a hold as base case but now prices 20% odds of an October hike, 30% in December. The 10Y at 4.59% is the bond expression of that hawkish pricing. Powell's term expires in May, adding leadership-gap and policy-tone-shift fears that directly fuel the duration selloff.

Sources: CNBC (Warsh) · Yahoo Finance · 24/7 Wall St
Macro · US-China

Trump-Xi May 14 summit — "stabilization, no breakthrough"

China committed to 200 Boeing aircraft + US agri/beef imports, and Trump claimed "the word tariff didn't come up," but summer tariff risk remains. No deal on NVIDIA chips to China — only a separate pre-existing H200 partial approval to select Chinese tech firms remains as residual semi support. The "trade truce extension + dispute management" is ceremonial at best; the market read it as effectively no-deal.

Sources: Atlantic Council · CNBC (Trump-Xi) · Euronews
Macro · Geopolitics·Energy

US-Iran 1-page MOU stalled, WTI intraday $109 closes at $101

Trump envoys Witkoff and Kushner are negotiating a 14-point MOU, but Trump rejected Iran's counterproposal (May 11) and Iran responded "never bow." A quick resolution of Hormuz control + nuclear enrichment + sanctions relief is unlikely. WTI spiked to $109 intraday on May 15 before closing $101.11 — amplitude was already large into the weekend. A single headline on May 18 can drive index volatility at the open.

Sources: Al Jazeera · Bloomberg (Iran deal) · CNBC
Technical · OPEX

May 15 monthly OPEX unwind aftermath

May 15 was the US May monthly OPEX. Expiry brought index pinning, dealer gamma hedging, and end-of-session unwind flows all at once. Because it landed right after ATH, call-side profit-taking and put-side hedges unwound together, contributing to the -1%+ acceleration. May 18, the first trading day after OPEX, typically sees fresh hedge re-positioning and runs hotter than usual on intraday range.

Sources: Regime Analysis (OPEX) · SpotGamma
▸ May 18 Open Scenario Tree
Below are scenario estimates grounded in the May 15 close, weekend macro, and OPEX aftermath — not confirmed forecasts.
Scenario A · Bounce (medium odds)

"Oversold + Warsh pricing already priced in"

Conditions: WTI settles around $100, 10Y eases below 4.55%, Iran headlines stay quiet. Then semis (NVDA/AMD/MU/INTC) gap up and lead S&P/Nasdaq +0.5% to +1%. A positive BIDU print on May 18 gives China-exposed names an extra boost. VIX 18 → 17 confirms the move.

Scenario B · Range (high odds)

"Macro burden persists but no fresh crash trigger"

Conditions: clean headlines + WTI in $100–$105 box. Sector rotation is the story — semis/tech weak, energy/materials/healthcare defending. Dow attempts but fails to retake 50k; range-bound close. Market waits for HD (5/19) and WMT (5/20) earnings. As an OPEX-aftermath day, intraday wide but close narrow.

Scenario C · Further drop (medium odds)

"Iran headline + Warsh hawkish soundbite = double punch"

Conditions: WTI back over $105 + Warsh or another FOMC member hawkish remark + Iran talks collapse headline. Then 10Y above 4.6%, Nasdaq -1%+ further. Dow tests 49,000 support. The selloff transmits to Asia for May 19 with a gap-down. VIX 20+ is the line between normal vol and panic.

BOTTOM LINE · One Line for May 18

May 15 wasn't simple profit-take — it was a "Warsh hawkish + Trump-Xi nothing-burger + Iran stall" macro triple-punch hitting at once. None of the three variables resolved over the weekend, so betting on a pure oversold bounce carries more uncertainty than usual.

Four checkpoints: ① First 30 minutes of 10Y, VIX, and the dollar — all calming = Scenario A; any one re-escalating = Scenario C. ② BIDU earnings reaction sets the tone for China-exposed names. ③ Warsh's first soundbite as Chair instantly re-prices duration names. ④ WTI $100 vs $105 — which side breaks.

Korea link: The US -1% close + macro stack already pressured the May 18 Korea open (Korea -6.12% on May 15 same day). US Scenario A reviving on May 18 → Korea gap up on May 19; B/C → range/weakness extends. "Did the US deliver a bounce?" is the prerequisite for Korea's recovery.

Traps to avoid: ① Don't read post-OPEX intraday range as trend. ② No statistical "minus 1.2% must bounce" bets — trend reversal is late while macro variables stay alive. ③ Warsh hawkish pricing still has room to deepen in bonds.
