Today's Verdict

Thursday's session was a tale of two markets. Headline indices closed nearly unchanged—S&P 500 -0.05%, Nasdaq -0.24%, Dow -0.09%—but beneath the surface, a violent rotation punished semiconductors while lifting cloud software and healthcare. The catalyst: China's Commerce Ministry issued fresh restrictions barring state-backed enterprises from purchasing US chip-manufacturing equipment, hitting Applied Materials -6.84%, KLA -6.52%, and Lam Research -5.98%. The SOX index plunged -5.29%, its worst decline since March. Yet Microsoft surged +5.71% on 32% Azure revenue growth, and Apple rallied +3.14% on strong iPhone 18 pre-order data, rescuing the broader market from a deeper selloff. Healthcare led all 11 GICS sectors with XLV +3.03% as Eli Lilly jumped +4.87% on obesity-drug trial success. Treasury yields eased—10Y fell 8bp to 4.37%—as safe-haven demand absorbed the China shock. The message: mega-cap rotation is alive, but semiconductor trade exposure is now a live risk premium.

📊 Today's Defining Theme — China Chip Curbs Trigger 5% SOX Wipeout, Cloud Rescues Indices

Thursday belonged to a single headline: China's new semiconductor-equipment purchasing restrictions. The Ministry of Commerce directive, aimed at US-made lithography, deposition, and inspection tools, immediately repriced semiconductor-equipment stocks. Applied Materials, with 28% revenue exposure to China, fell -6.84%; KLA dropped -6.52%; Lam Research shed -5.98%. The SOX index, which tracks 30 semiconductor stocks, plunged -5.29% to 13,203.57, erasing two weeks of gains. Nvidia fell -1.64% and Broadcom -3.67% as investors extrapolated the restrictions to AI chip exports. Micron declined -3.45% after DRAM spot prices dropped 2.1% week-over-week, compounding China demand fears.

Cloud and Healthcare Offset the Wreckage

Microsoft's +5.71% surge provided the day's counterweight. Management commentary highlighting 32% Azure year-over-year revenue growth and expanded AI infrastructure capex triggered a Wedbush price-target raise to $440. Amazon climbed +2.50% after AWS secured an $8.2 billion, 5-year cloud contract with the US Department of Defense. Apple added +3.14% on reports that iPhone 18 pre-orders rose 18% versus the prior generation, with Foxconn announcing Zhengzhou factory expansion. Together, these three mega-caps contributed roughly +60 points to the S&P 500, offsetting -40 points of semiconductor drag. Healthcare joined the rally: Eli Lilly jumped +4.87% after Phase 3 obesity-drug trials showed 23.5% average weight loss, exceeding expectations and prompting plans to file for expanded FDA indications. UnitedHealth rose +2.38% on raised 2026 EPS guidance to $33.50 versus $32.10 consensus. XLV Healthcare closed +3.03%, the best-performing GICS sector.

Implications for Korean Markets

Friday's KOSPI open faces conflicting forces. Samsung Electronics (005930) and SK hynix (000660) will feel immediate pressure from the SOX -5.29% crash, especially given their own China revenue exposure (Samsung ~20%, SK hynix ~25%). Expect gap-down opens for both. Yet the cloud strength—MSFT +5.71%, AMZN +2.50%—supports Korean IT services and data-center plays like Naver (035420) and Kakao (035720).
