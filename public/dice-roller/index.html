How to use the dice roller

  1. Add the players. Enter everyone who's rolling — or just roll solo for a quick number.
  2. Tap to roll. The dice tumble and land on a random face from 1 to 6 for each player.
  3. Read the result. The highest roll wins; the lowest can take the penalty you agreed on.
  4. Share it. Send the outcome to friends with a one-tap link so nobody argues with the dice.
