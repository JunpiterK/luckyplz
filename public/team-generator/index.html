Frequently asked questions

Is the team generator free?

Yes — completely free with no sign-up. Enter names, choose your teams, and generate.

How does it split people into teams?

Names are shuffled randomly and dealt out evenly across the number of teams you pick, so each team is fair and balanced.

What if the count doesn't divide evenly?

The remainder is spread one per team, so team sizes differ by at most one person.

Can I choose how many teams?

Yes. Set the number of teams (or people per team) before generating and the tool balances everyone across them.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It's mobile-first and runs in any phone, tablet, or desktop browser with nothing to install.

Can I re-roll or share the teams?

Yes. Generate again for a fresh split, and share the result with a one-tap link so everyone sees the same teams.
