How to use the wheel spinner

  1. Add your options. Type each name, choice, or task into the wheel — people's names, restaurants, chores, prizes, anything.
  2. Spin. Tap the wheel or the Spin button. It accelerates, slows down, and lands on one segment at random.
  3. Read the winner. The selected option is highlighted with the pointer. Spin again as many times as you like.
  4. Share it. Send the result to friends with a one-tap link so everyone sees the same outcome.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wheel spinner free?

Yes — completely free with no sign-up. Open the page, type your options, and spin.

Is the spin really random?

Yes. Each spin uses the browser's random number generator, so every option has an equal chance of being picked.

How many names can I add?

As many as you like. The wheel resizes each segment automatically so all entries stay readable.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It's mobile-first and runs in any phone, tablet, or desktop browser with nothing to install.

Can I share the result?

Yes. After a spin you can share the outcome with a one-tap link, including KakaoTalk, so friends see the same result.

Do I need an account?

No. Spinning needs no login — accounts only unlock optional social and multiplayer features.
