  • Auto-replies — none. If you don't hear back in 3 days, your email may have hit Gmail's spam filter; please send again from a different address.

Privacy & data requests

We honor GDPR (EU/EEA) and CCPA (California) data rights. Email [PRIVACY] with one of these requests:

  • Right to access — we'll send a JSON export of any data tied to your account within 30 days. (For most users this is just login email + game scores; we don't keep much.)
  • Right to deletion — we'll erase your account, scores, and any saved presets within 30 days. Confirmation email when done. Anonymous game data (e.g. an aggregated leaderboard count) is unrecoverable, but personal IDs are wiped.
  • Right to correction — wrong nickname / wrong email tied to your record? We'll fix it.
  • Right to portability — included in the access export.
  • Right to opt out of sale — we don't sell user data. There is nothing to opt out of, but we acknowledge the request.

If you're not sure which right applies, just describe what you want done and we'll figure out the right framework. Identity verification: a reply from the same email tied to your account is enough; for unattached cases, a one-time confirmation link.

What this inbox is NOT

  • Not a play partner finder. If you want to find someone to play multiplayer with, ping a friend, then create a room — game, not email.
  • Not a tournament organizer. Lucky Please is a quick-decision tool, not a competitive ladder. Score reset / cheat-investigation requests will be politely declined.
  • Not technical support for your work laptop. If a corporate firewall blocks our domain, we can confirm what we serve, but the unblock has to happen on your IT side.

Office address (legal / mailing)
